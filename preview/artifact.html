Before booking

Important Notices Before Booking

Please read this before you book.

Required Items

  • ID submission is required for all participants (passport, residence card, driver's license, or My Number card).
  • Vintage shopping & home cooking: 18 years and older.
  • JDM car experience: 20 years and older (driver's license required).
  • Full payment at booking. The cancellation policy applies.
  • Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the experience; 50% after that; 100% on the day.

Health & Allergies

  • Home cooking: allergies and religious dietary restrictions must be declared in advance.
  • JDM car experience: pre-existing conditions and medication use must be declared in advance.

Photo & Video Recording

  • Photos and video during the experience require the host's permission.
  • Posting on social media requires individual consent.

Code of Conduct

The following are prohibited during all experiences:

  • Alcohol and drugs
  • Violence and threats
  • Unauthorized photography or recording
  • Theft and damage
  • Sexual harassment
  • Political or religious solicitation
  • Commercial activity

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still wondering about something? Start here.

QIs ID submission required?
A

Yes, ID submission is required for all participants for safety. Please submit a passport, residence card, driver's license, or My Number card.

QCan I cancel?
A

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the experience. A 50% cancellation fee applies after that, and 100% on the day.

QWhat languages are supported?
A

English, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and French. LINE customer support is also available.

QIs it a small group?
A

Yes, small groups of 1-4 people for personalized attention.

QWhat happens in case of rain?
A

Vintage shopping and the JDM car experience will be changed to indoor-focused plans. Home cooking is indoors, so there is no impact.

QI have allergies, can you accommodate?
A

Yes, please declare allergies at booking. The host will adjust the ingredients.

QCan I take photos?
A

Yes, taking photos during the experience is possible. However, the host's permission is required, and individual consent is obtained for social media posting.

QWhat payment methods are accepted?
A

Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Amex), LINE Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
